# Failures and weather extras

**Status: pinned.** Small items, each useful on its own.

## Failures to practise

- **ESC low-voltage cut-off**: many plane ESCs cut or soft-cut the motor at ~3.0–3.2 V/cell. With
  the new cold-pack model, a punch on a cold day can trigger it mid-climb.
- **Motor or ESC failure** on one motor: quads tumble, a hex or octo can survive; planes go
  glider.
- **Prop damage**: a chipped prop brings vibration and less thrust.
- **GPS**: glitch (position jump), loss, jamming / spoofing.
- **Compass interference** near the power line or from high current.
- **Brown-out** of the receiver or VTX from a sagging BEC.
- **Cell imbalance**: one weak cell sags early.
- **Servo failure** on a plane: an elevon stuck, flutter.

## Weather extras

- **Thermals and sink**: columns of rising air on sunny days, sink between them.
- **Mountain wave and rotor** (with terrain maps).
- **Gust fronts and microbursts**: a sudden wind shift and downdraft, practising the go-around.
- **Night**: lighting, nav lights on the aircraft, LOS orientation in the dark.
- **Sun glare**: losing LOS when the aircraft crosses the sun.
- **Freezing rain** as its own type, distinct from drizzle (larger drops, faster glaze).
